✍️ The Ultimate Guide to Conditionals

👥 Visitors: 0

Conditional sentences are used to speculate about what could happen, what might have happened, and what we wish would happen.

Type Usage Formula
Zero Scientific Facts / General Truths If + Simple Present, Simple Present
1st Possible Future Predictions If + Simple Present, Will + Verb 1
2nd Imaginary / Unreal Situations If + Simple Past, Would + Verb 1
3rd Regrets About the Past If + Past Perfect, Would have + V3

💡 Deep Dive: Mixed Conditionals

This is the "Pro" level. We mix the past with the present.

Formula: If + Past Perfect, Would + Verb 1

Example: "If I had studied harder at school, I would have a better job now."

Explanation: A past action (studying) has an ongoing impact on a present situation (job).

⚠️ Note on "Unless":

Unless means "If not."
"I won't go unless it stops raining" means the same as "I won't go if it doesn't stop raining."
